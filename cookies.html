“Cookies” and similar technologies (like local storage) let a website remember small pieces of information in your browser. This page explains what Do Step Inn Central uses. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

01 What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device; local storage is a similar browser mechanism. They can be strictly necessary (needed for the site to work) or optional (analytics, personalisation, advertising). We use only the strictly necessary kind.

02 What We Store

Our own site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The only thing we store is a single local-storage entry to remember your cookie choice:

Name
dsi-cookie
Type
Local storage (essential)
Purpose
Remembers that you’ve seen and answered the cookie notice
Duration
Until you clear your browser storage

03 Third-Party Embeds

A few features rely on third parties. The booking calendar and the map load only after you agree (or click their explicit “Load” button) — nothing external is requested by default:

  • uphotel booking engine — loads on request (after consent); may set functional cookies while you use the booking calendar.
  • OpenStreetMap — loads on request; the Contact-page map fetches tiles from OpenStreetMap.
  • Open-Meteo — live weather; no cookies, no tracking.
  • Fonts — self-hosted on our own servers; no data is sent to Google.

04 Legal Basis & Consent

Strictly necessary storage is used on the basis of our legitimate interest in a working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and does not require consent. The booking calendar and the map are loaded only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — you can decline and still use the rest of the site. Fonts are self-hosted, so they need no consent.

05 How to Control Cookies

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you clear existing data and refuse new storage. Please note that blocking storage may affect features such as the map or the booking calendar. Guidance is available in your browser’s help pages (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

06 Changes

If we add new cookies or services, we’ll update this page and, where required, ask for your consent. The version published here applies.
