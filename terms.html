These Terms govern the booking, use and payment of accommodation and related services. Deviating or additional terms only apply if we have agreed to them in writing. By completing a booking, you accept these Terms.

02 Reservations

You can reserve through our website booking engine, by email or by phone. A reservation request is your binding offer to conclude an accommodation contract on the terms shown at the time of booking.

03 Booking Confirmation

The contract is concluded when we send you a written booking confirmation (usually by email). Please check it on receipt and tell us immediately if anything is incorrect. If you don’t receive a confirmation, your booking is not yet binding — contact us.

04 Prices

Prices are stated in euros (EUR) per bed or room per night, as shown during booking, and include statutory VAT. A local tourist/city tax may be added where legally required Client confirmation required. The price stated in your booking confirmation is the price that applies.

05 Payment Terms

Accepted payment methods and the time of payment (e.g. prepayment at booking, or payment on arrival) are shown during the booking process Client confirmation required. We may require a valid card to guarantee the reservation.

06 Cancellation Policy

Cancellation and no-show conditions depend on the rate you book and are shown before you confirm. Exact free-cancellation deadlines and any cancellation or no-show fees Client confirmation required. Non-refundable rates, where offered, are marked as such at booking.

07 Check-in

Do Step Inn Central uses contactless self check-in: you receive your door and bed codes before arrival, so you can arrive at any time. The earliest check-in time Client confirmation required. A valid photo ID is required, and guests must complete the statutory guest registration (Meldegesetz).

08 Check-out

Check-out is by 11:00 on the day of departure. Later departure may be possible on request, subject to availability and a possible fee Client confirmation required. Luggage storage is available if you leave before your onward travel.

Our house rules are displayed on site and form part of these Terms. They exist to keep the hostel safe, clean and welcoming for everyone. We may refuse admission to, or ask to leave, guests who seriously or repeatedly breach them, without a refund.

Please treat the property, staff and fellow travellers with respect, keep shared spaces such as the kitchen tidy, use your locker and keypad to secure your belongings, and report any damage or technical issue as soon as you notice it.

Do Step Inn Central is a non-smoking property, including all rooms and indoor shared spaces. Smoking (and vaping) is only permitted in designated outdoor areas Client confirmation required. A cleaning fee may apply for smoking indoors Client confirmation required.

13 Alcohol & Illegal Drugs

Illegal drugs are strictly prohibited on the premises. Alcohol may be consumed responsibly in permitted areas; behaviour that endangers or disturbs others may lead to removal from the property without refund.

Quiet hours apply so everyone can rest. Exact quiet-hour times Client confirmation required. Please keep noise to a minimum in dorms and corridors at all times.
