This policy explains how Kern Beherbergungsbetriebs GmbH processes personal data in connection with the website dostepinncentral.at and stays at Do Step Inn Central. We only process what we need, and we never sell your data.

02 Overview

We process personal data to run our website, take and manage bookings, meet our legal obligations as an accommodation provider, and respond to your enquiries. Processing follows the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG).

03 Data We Process

  • Booking data — name, contact details, dates, room/rate, number of guests, payment-related data handled by our booking provider.
  • Guest registration data — the details required by the Austrian Registration Act (Meldegesetz).
  • Communication data — the content of emails, calls or messages you send us.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser/device information and timestamps in server logs when you visit the site.

04 Legal Bases

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — to process and fulfil your booking.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — e.g. guest registration and retention of accounting records.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — secure, reliable operation of our website.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — where we ask for it, e.g. optional cookies; you can withdraw it any time.

05 Hosting & Server Logs

Our website is hosted on Vercel (Vercel Inc.). When you visit, the host automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, the pages requested, and timestamps, to deliver the site securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f)). A data processing agreement is in place with the provider Client confirmation required.

06 Booking Engine

Reservations are handled through our integrated booking system provided by uphotel (ibe.uphotel.agency / myibe.com). When you make a booking, the data you enter is processed to complete your reservation and manage your stay (Art. 6(1)(b)). The provider acts as our processor under a data processing agreement Client confirmation required — provider legal name & DPA. Payment data is handled by the relevant payment provider Client confirmation required.

07 Guest Registration

As an accommodation provider we are legally required to register guests (Austrian Meldegesetz). The data collected for this purpose is processed to meet that legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and retained for the statutory period.

08 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website itself sets no tracking or advertising cookies. We store a single entry in your browser’s local storage to remember your cookie choice. Some embedded third-party services may set cookies or process your IP address — details and controls are on our Cookie Policy.

09 Third-Party Services

Some features rely on third parties. The booking calendar and the map load only after your consent (or an explicit click) — until then, no request is made to them and no IP address is transmitted. Our fonts are self-hosted, so no data is sent to Google.

  • uphotel booking engine — loaded on request to complete your booking; see “Booking Engine” above.
  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation) — loaded on request for the map on our Contact page.
  • Open-Meteo — live Vienna weather. No cookies, no account, no tracking.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. If this changes, we’ll update this policy and obtain consent where required.

10 Contacting Us

If you email or call us, we process your details and message to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f)). We keep this correspondence only as long as needed.

11 Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law (e.g. accounting and registration retention periods under Austrian law). Exact periods Client confirmation required.

12 International Transfers

Some providers (e.g. hosting or fonts) may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where they do, transfers are safeguarded by appropriate measures such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision Client confirmation required.

13 Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21) to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. To exercise any right, email office@dostepinn.at.

14 Right to Complain

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna — dsb.gv.at.

15 Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS). No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

16 Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or the law. The version published here applies. Please check back from time to time.

17 Contact

Questions about your data or this policy? Email office@dostepinn.at or call +43 1 890 23 51. Company details are on our Imprint; see also our Cookie Policy.
