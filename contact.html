Good to know

FAQ

How do I get in with a code?

After booking you'll receive door and bed codes by email. There's no reception desk to queue at — you can arrive any time, day or night.

Can I drop my bag before check-in?

Yes. Luggage storage is available, so you can explore Vienna the moment you arrive and grab your bag after check-out too.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes — a fully equipped self-catering kitchen with a big fridge. Cook together; it's the social heart of the place.

How far is the station and the airport?

Wien Hauptbahnhof is a 3-minute walk (you're directly above it). The airport is about 15 minutes by train (CAT/S7) from the same station.

Is it safe?

Full-day security, lockable pods with private keypad codes, and secure entry. Guests consistently rate the location and cleanliness highly.
